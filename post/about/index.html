Hello! Welcome to Celia’s Tennis World.

This is more than just a website; it is a living diary documenting the journey of Ding Xiya (Celia), a 10-year-old girl chasing her tennis dreams.

The Origin: A Special 10th Birthday Gift In December 2025, to celebrate Celia's 10th birthday, this independent website was officially launched. Just as every stroke on the court requires focus and power, this digital space holds our infinite hopes for her future. We created this archive to preserve these precious moments of her childhood and adolescence.

Witnessing Sweat and Glory From the intense summer drills at the Moganshan Training Camp to the competitive courts of the ASICS Junior Tennis Tour Finals (CTJ-B600); from the joy of lifting her first C400 Champion trophy to every gritty match at the Qizhong Tennis Center. Here, you will see how Celia nurtures her talent with hard work and learns valuable lessons from every victory and defeat.

A Promising Future Tennis is a marathon, and being ten years old is just the starting line. Celia’s journey has just begun. The road ahead may have challenges, but it is filled with infinite possibilities.

Thank you for visiting celiatennis.online. Join us in witnessing the rise of a future star!
